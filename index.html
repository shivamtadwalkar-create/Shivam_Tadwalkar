Solar heat, kept for after sunset

Engineering Co-op, Exowatt, Austin, Texas. Fall 2026.

Exowatt builds modular systems that capture sunlight as heat, store it, and turn it back into electricity whenever it's needed, day or night. I work on the engineering that takes its thermal energy storage hardware from CAD to the shop floor.

The specifics stay in-house. What I can show is the physics everyone in this field designs around: collect heat while the sun is up, hold it, and spend it steadily through the night.

Bio-inspired shells for spinal implants

Student Technician with Dr. Ashutosh Agrawal, EnMed, J. Mike Walker '66 Department of Mechanical Engineering, Texas A&M University. Since March 2026.

Torene is a bio-inspired, high-genus architecture: concentric shells joined by toroidal junctions, 3D printed for spinal interbody fusion cages. I'm characterizing how it buckles, vibrates and absorbs impact, in three parallel workstreams headed for journal submission. Impact is my current focus: a velocity sweep against plain plates to find the energy where a plain plate fractures and Torene survives.

IDAC India

Coupled thermal-structural FEA on heat exchanger base frames, iterated in ANSYS and SolidWorks until the weight came out without eating into the safety margin.

  • Heat reaching the frame through its saddles adds thermal stress, and hot steel is weaker steel.
  • Iterative resizing trimmed material where the stress didn't need it.
18%weight reduction on heat exchanger frames

How this site works

Everything here is computed live in your browser, with no libraries. The name at the top runs three solvers on one grid: a stable-fluids wind tunnel with vorticity confinement, an explicit heat equation in which the letters act as a thermal battery, and a damped wave equation with the letters as walls. The IDAC frame is a real truss solved with the direct stiffness method and resized by fully stressed design. The gauge chart computes live process capability, the MDO plot runs NSGA-II, and the perception demo is a running extended Kalman filter. Press T for thermal view.
